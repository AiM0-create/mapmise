## Mapmise 0.1.0a1 — first public pre-release

Mapmise determines which open geospatial datasets an analysis requires, verifies that they are usable for the
stated area and period, acquires only the area of interest, and delivers an organised, dated and fully documented
project. It runs entirely on your computer, with no account, hosted service or API key.

This is an **alpha** release, published to gather feedback from practitioners. It is functional end to end on real
data; interfaces and file formats may still change.

### Highlights

- **A question in, a dataset out.** Place, period and analysis are read from a plain-language question. The place
  is resolved to an administrative boundary, and each data requirement is stated with its rationale.
- **Feasibility before acquisition.** Scenes are selected and assessed per tile and time window. Windows in which
  optical imagery cannot provide a clear view are reported as infeasible and paired with a cloud-independent
  alternative; products that do not span the requested years are combined with others that do.
- **Twenty-four open sources**, including Sentinel-1 and -2, Landsat, MODIS, Copernicus DEM, ESA WorldCover,
  JRC Global Surface Water, Hansen Global Forest Change, SoilGrids, WorldPop, CHIRPS and OpenStreetMap. Every entry
  is verified live against its provider.
- **Area-only acquisition** into Cloud-Optimised GeoTIFFs and GeoPackages in a single projection, with a static STAC
  catalogue, a SHA-256 checksum per file, and a plain-language acquisition report.
- **Desktop application** for Windows, macOS and Linux, in its own window and designed to Apple's Human Interface
  principles — ask, review the plan, acquire, and open the project in QGIS. No Python installation is required.
- **Offline language understanding** through a compact embedded model (23 MB). On a held-out evaluation it raised
  correct understanding from 11 of 17 questions to 16 of 17. It selects only among existing rules and always states
  its reasoning.
- **Personal library and recipes.** Files already held are reused when this is provably equivalent to a fresh
  download; a project can be shared as a small recipe and rebuilt with checksum verification.

### Installation notes

These installers are not yet code-signed, so the operating system asks for confirmation on first launch:

- **Windows:** choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **macOS:** open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**.

Each build was produced by the repository's public release workflow and tested on its own operating system before
publication, including a live acquisition. An installation can be verified at any time with `mapmise-cli selftest`.

### Known limitations

- Understanding is English-only and limited to the analyses described by the ask rules.
- No soil-moisture source yet; the open products require a NASA Earthdata login.
- The area of interest is defined by a place name or a boundary file; drawing on the map is not yet supported.
- OpenStreetMap building queries are limited to 2,000 km².
- The first plan for a new area may take up to a minute while catalogues are queried.
