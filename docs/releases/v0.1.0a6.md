## Mapmise 0.1.0a6

- **Sensible periods for questions about change.** A question such as *Expansion of Noida* or *Forest loss in
  Wayanad* with no dates now covers the last ten years (five for lakes and reservoirs) instead of the last twelve
  months, which was too short to show change and ended beyond the newest yearly land-cover products. The plan
  states the default, and naming a period changes it.
