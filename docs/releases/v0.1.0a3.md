## Mapmise 0.1.0a3

### NASA Earthdata

Mapmise can now use datasets from NASA's protected Earthdata archive with your own free access token:

- **HLS** — Harmonized Landsat and Sentinel-2 surface reflectance, 30 m, on a common grid (2013– and 2015–).
- **OPERA DSWx-HLS** — ready-made maps of surface water for every clear scene, 30 m (2023–). Flood and water-body
  questions now ask for these maps.

Add a token under **Settings → NASA Earthdata**, or with `mapmise earthdata login`. Use a token generated at
urs.earthdata.nasa.gov, never your password. The token stays on your computer, in a file only your account can read,
and is sent only to NASA; it never appears in projects, reports or recipes. Without a token, plans say which
requirements these datasets would have met.
